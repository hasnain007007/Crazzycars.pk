## T1 — ABS vs real carbon fiber (PRIORITY)

**Proposed slug:** `abs-vs-real-carbon-fiber-car-accessories-pakistan`  
**Title:** ABS vs Real Carbon Fiber Car Accessories in Pakistan: What’s Actually on the Listing  
**Meta title (≤60 branded):** `ABS vs Real Carbon Fiber Accessories | CrazzyCars`  
**Meta description:** Most “carbon” trims sold online in Pakistan are ABS with a carbon-style finish—not woven fiber. Here’s how to read titles, photos, and fitment before you order.  
**Excerpt:** If a listing says carbon fiber but the price looks like a trim kit, it is usually ABS with a carbon-look texture. This guide explains the difference—and how CrazzyCars labels it.  
**Categories:** Guides, Buying Tips  
**Tags:** abs vs carbon fiber, carbon fiber accessories pakistan, carbon look abs, real carbon fiber car trim  
**relatedProducts (suggest):** 2–3 high-traffic carbon-style ABS SKUs already FAQ’d (e.g. City/Civic steering trim, door handles)—not “real fiber” claims.

### Body

Pakistan’s accessory market uses “carbon fiber” loosely. On CrazzyCars, most interior and exterior trims sold as carbon-style are **ABS plastic with a carbon-look texture or print**—not a woven carbon-fiber panel bonded like a race part.

**Why shops say “carbon” anyway**  
Buyers search for that look. A snap-on gear-knob cover or door-handle set at everyday prices is almost never autoclave carbon. It is a moulded ABS piece finished to look like weave. That can still be a useful upgrade: lighter on the wallet, easier to fit, and honest when the listing says “carbon-style,” “carbon look,” or “ABS.”

**How to read a CrazzyCars listing**  
1. **Title and short description** — Prefer wording like carbon-style / carbon look / ABS. If something were real woven fiber, it should say so clearly and the price/weight would usually reflect it.  
2. **Photos** — Edge thickness, back side, and install clips often reveal ABS mouldings.  
3. **Vehicle compatibility table** — Material honesty does not replace fitment. Use the table on the product page (make/model/years) as the fitment source of truth.  
4. **FAQ on the product** — Many carbon-look PDPs already answer “Is it real carbon fiber?” with an ABS disclosure.

**What we do not claim**  
We do not invent “100% real carbon” language on ABS kits. Where older titles still say “carbon fiber” out of habit, treat the **description + FAQ + photos** as the honesty layer until titles are cleaned in a separate data-quality pass—not by silently rewriting SEO.

**Where to shop the look (with eyes open)**  
Browse [Carbon Fiber Accessories](/categories/carbon-fiber-accessories) and [Side Mirror Covers](/categories/side-mirror-covers). For styling context, see also [Side Mirror Covers & Carbon Fiber Accents](/blogs/side-mirror-covers-carbon-fiber-accents-guide).

**COD and delivery**  
Eligible items can use Cash on Delivery with delivery paid in advance. See [Cash on Delivery](/cash-on-delivery) and our [shipping policy](/shipping-policy).

2. Q: How do I know what I’m buying?

**Categories:** Guides, Buying Tips  
**Tags:** crazzycars returns, wrong item delivered, car accessories return policy pakistan, exchange only  
**relatedProducts:** none required.

### Body

Buying body kits, trims, and lights online only works if returns rules are clear. This is the plain-language version of what we already publish in policy and FAQ—not a new promise.

**When you get a full refund**  
Within **7 days** of delivery, if the item arrives **defective** or we shipped the **wrong item**, you are eligible for a **full refund**. In those cases we cover return shipping as stated in our returns policy.

**Change of mind**  
If the part is correct and undamaged but you changed your mind (wrong colour preference, ordered two styles, etc.), we offer an **exchange** for a different product or size—**not** a cash refund. That matches how many specialty accessory shops in Pakistan operate on COD orders.

**Fitment mistakes vs wrong item**  
Ordering the wrong year/generation when the listing’s vehicle table was clear is usually **not** “wrong item shipped.” Use the compatibility table (and, once live, our year/variant matching guide) before you confirm COD. If we sent a different SKU than you paid for, that **is** wrong-item territory.

**How to start a claim**  
Open the [Returns Policy](/returns-policy), or start from [FAQ](/faq). Keep unboxing photos, the order number, and a clear photo of the part label/packaging. WhatsApp support with those details speeds things up.

**Related reading**  
[Cash on Delivery vs Online Payment](/blogs/cod-vs-online-payment-car-accessories-pakistan) · [Cash on Delivery landing](/cash-on-delivery) · [Shipping policy](/shipping-policy)

**The rule (live store policy)**  
Delivery is **Rs. 250** for regular items, or **Rs. 500** when the order includes **bulky** items (splitters, side skirts, spoilers, floor mats, etc.). Shipping is paid **in advance**; the rest is Cash on Delivery where the item is COD-eligible. There is **no** order-value waiver for delivery.

**What usually triggers Rs. 500**  
Carts that include items flagged bulky in catalog—common on [Splitters & Side Skirts](/categories/splitters-side-skirts), [Spoilers & Diffusers](/categories/spoilers-diffusers), and [Floor Mats](/categories/floor-mats). If any line in the cart is bulky, checkout uses the bulky fee.

**Spoilers / special courier**  
Roof or trunk spoilers and Express (Daewoo) can use a **different courier rate shown at checkout**. Always trust the checkout line, not a screenshot from last month.

**How this pairs with COD**  
You still pay delivery up front (JazzCash / bank / listed methods), then pay for the goods on delivery when COD applies. Body kits named as body kits cannot use COD—see product FAQ and [Cash on Delivery](/cash-on-delivery).
