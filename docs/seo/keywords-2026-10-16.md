# CrazzyCars.pk: commercial keyword research (16 Oct 2026, PKT)

**Method.** I had no paid volume data, so no search volumes are given. Priority comes from evidence:
1. **Google autocomplete** (gl=pk, ~120 seed queries; raw output in `kw/ac.txt` and `kw/ac2.txt`).
2. **Competitor targeting** (theautofy, thecarguys, autozcare, izyanautoparts, aoneautos, megamotorsports, autojin, ckdmotors, lasersound, sehgalmotors, carbooth).
3. **SERP checks.**

High = exact phrase in PK autocomplete AND competitors have dedicated pages. Med = one of these. Low = weak or only implied.
The full list (96 rows) is in `keywords-2026-10-16.csv`.

**Key findings**
- Pakistani buyers search in **English, model-first, with "price in pakistan"** (for example "yaris floor mats price in pakistan" or "civic x body kit price in pakistan"). Roman Urdu product queries ("gari ki seat cover", "gari ke mats", "gari ka body kit") return **no autocomplete**. The one useful Roman Urdu word is **"poshish"** (seat covers), which competitors use.
- Model aliases matter: **Civic X / 10th gen / 11th gen / Reborn / Rebirth**, **Corolla X / Altis / Grande / GLi**, plus year numbers (2015, 2018, 2021, 2022).
- **Biggest catalogue gaps:** seat covers (0 products), 7D/9D mats (crazzycars only has TPE), H4 LED bulbs, Alto/Cultus/Mehran body kits, sun shades (1 product), Wagon R mats.
