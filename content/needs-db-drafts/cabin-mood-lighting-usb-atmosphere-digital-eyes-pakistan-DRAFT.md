<h2>Safety filter</h2>
<ul>
<li>Nothing flashing in the driver’s forward field of view on highways.</li>
<li>Keep roof lasers away from eyes at close range.</li>
<li>Unplug USB toys when parking for days — phantom drain is real on weak batteries.</li>
<li>Never mount heavy gadgets on airbag zones or soft dash tops that cook in sun.</li>
<li>Humidifier water + electronics: use a stable cupholder.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Humidifier / atmosphere setup</h2>
<ol>
<li>Place in a deep cupholder; confirm it can’t tip under braking.</li>
<li>Fill with distilled water only; empty when unused for days.</li>
<li>Start with low light modes at night; increase only if not reflecting in the windshield.</li>
<li>Route the USB cable so it doesn’t interfere with the gear lever or handbrake.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Digital eyes placement</h2>
<ul>
<li>Passenger footwell or rear cabin panels for parked content.</li>
<li>Avoid windshield-adjacent flash toward oncoming traffic.</li>
<li>Prefer event/meet use over daily highway companions.</li>
</ul>

<h2>FAQ</h2>
<h3>Will a USB humidifier damage electronics?</h3>
<p>Only if it leaks. Sit it stable, don’t overfill, and empty when unused.</p>
<h3>Are digital eyes legal?</h3>
<p>Decorative cabin panels are generally fine; don’t confuse them with external signalling lights.</p>
<h3>Battery drain?</h3>
<p>Unplug when the car sits. Prefer sockets that lose power with ignition where possible.</p>
